Pickleball Court Flooring Solution

Complete pickleball court flooring systems for indoor and outdoor applications, designed to provide consistent ball bounce, reliable grip, player comfort and long-lasting performance.

Typical Court System

  • Prepared Concrete or Asphalt Base - stable and level foundation
  • Primer / Base Coat - improves adhesion and surface preparation
  • Acrylic Resurfacer - helps create a smooth, uniform playing surface
  • Cushion Layer - Optional - provides additional comfort and impact reduction
  • Textured Acrylic Colour Coats - durable, slip-resistant playing finish
  • Precision Court Line Marking - clearly defined pickleball playing lines

Key Benefits

Excellent ball response, non-slip textured finish, UV and weather resistance, low maintenance, customizable court colours, and suitable for both new courts and resurfacing projects.

Applications

Residential courts, schools and universities, sports clubs, hotels and resorts, community facilities, commercial pickleball centres and multi-sport courts.

From sub-base preparation and flooring installation to line marking, nets and court accessories, we can provide a complete turnkey pickleball court solution.
