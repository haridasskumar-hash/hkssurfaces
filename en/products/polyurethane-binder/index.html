Polyurethane Binder

Reliable bonding for seamless rubber surfaces

Single-component, solvent-free polyurethane binder for SBR and EPDM rubber granules. Its moisture-curing formula provides strong adhesion, flexibility, and durable performance for cast-in-situ safety and sports flooring.

Key Benefits

  • Strong, flexible adhesion
  • Low-VOC, solvent-free formula
  • Excellent weather resistance
  • Suitable for SBR and EPDM
  • Practical working time
  • Aromatic and UV-stable aliphatic options
  • Multiple packaging sizes

Choose Your Binder

Aromatic Polyurethane Binder

A cost-effective option for everyday rubber flooring applications. Models: MDI-88, MDI-6117. Aromatic binders may develop a temporary yellow tint under strong UV exposure. This can gradually reduce with normal surface wear.

Aliphatic Polyurethane Binder

A premium transparent binder with superior UV resistance. It helps preserve rubber color and minimize discoloration. Model: HDI-10. Recommended for blue, gray, white, and other colors requiring long-term stability.

Technical Information

Working and curing times may vary with temperature, humidity, materials, and installation method.

  • Viscosity: 2,000-3,500 cps at 25°C
  • Solid content: >=98%
  • VOC: <2%
  • Mixing ratio: SBR 1:7; EPDM 1:5
  • Pot life: 30-60 minutes
  • Curing time: Approximately 24 hours at 25°C
  • Packaging: 25 kg, 120 kg, or customized
  • Shelf life: 12 months unopened
  • Storage: 10-30°C
