Rubber Safety Tiles

High-performance rubber safety tiles and pavers for playgrounds, sports facilities, recreational areas, walkways and specialist applications. Manufactured from durable recycled SBR rubber with optional EPDM colour finishes.

Our Rubber Tile Solutions

Playground Safety Tiles

Shock-absorbing rubber tiles designed to help reduce the risk of injury from falls while providing a comfortable, slip-resistant playground surface. Best for: Playgrounds, schools, childcare centres and recreational areas.

Interlocking Rubber Tiles

Modular rubber tiles with interlocking edges for fast installation and easy replacement. Available in different thicknesses according to application requirements. Best for: Gyms, fitness areas, sports facilities, walkways and multi-purpose spaces.

Rubber Pavers

Heavy-duty rubber paving combining the appearance of conventional paving with the resilience and comfort of rubber. Best for: Walkways, patios, landscape areas, golf facilities, recreational areas and high-traffic spaces.

Ballistic Rubber Tiles & Blocks

High-density specialist rubber products designed to absorb projectile energy and help reduce ricochet within appropriately engineered ballistic systems. Best for: Shooting ranges and specialist training facilities.

Why Choose HKS Rubber Safety Tiles?

  • Excellent impact & shock absorption
  • Anti-slip and comfortable underfoot
  • Durable and weather resistant
  • Helps reduce noise and vibration
  • Modular installation & easy replacement
  • Indoor and outdoor applications
  • Multiple thicknesses and configurations
  • SBR and EPDM colour-finish options
  • Recycled rubber options available

Colours & Finishes

Choose from black recycled rubber, pigmented colour rubber or EPDM colour finishes to complement the functional and aesthetic requirements of your project.
