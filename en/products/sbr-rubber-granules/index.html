SBR Rubber Granules

Reliable | Shock Absorbing | Cost-Effective

SBR Rubber Granules are durable recycled rubber materials widely used for playgrounds, sports surfaces and recreational areas. In wet-pour flooring systems, SBR is commonly used as the shock-absorbing base layer beneath Green EPDM, providing cushioning, resilience and cost-effective system thickness.

Key Benefits

  • Excellent Shock Absorption
  • Durable & Resilient
  • Cost-Effective
  • Noise & Vibration Reduction
  • Recycled Rubber
  • Multiple Sizes & Types

Types of SBR & Colours

01 - Black SBR Granules | Black

Standard SBR granules ideal for shock-absorbing base layers beneath EPDM playground and sports flooring.

02 - Color-Coated SBR | Various Colours

SBR granules with a coloured coating, providing an economical option for selected coloured rubber surfacing applications.

03 - Bluffing SBR | Black

Elongated shredded SBR rubber suitable for cushioning, sports and specialised rubber applications.

04 - SBR Mulch | Brown & Other Colours

Larger rubber pieces with a mulch-like appearance, ideal for playground surrounds, landscaping, pathways and recreational areas.

Applications

Playgrounds | EPDM Wet-Pour Base Layers | Sports Surfaces | Running Tracks | Outdoor Fitness | Rubber Flooring | Artificial Turf | Landscaping
