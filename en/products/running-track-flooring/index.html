Green Running Tracks by HKS Surfaces

HKS Surfaces delivers durable, high-performance athletics surfacing for schools, stadiums and sports facilities. Each system can be tailored to suit the required performance, budget, usage level and site conditions.

Running Track Systems

Spray-Coat Track System

A cost-effective, permeable system for training tracks and multi-use facilities. It combines a resilient SBR rubber base with a spray-applied EPDM wear layer for dependable grip, comfort and everyday performance. Typical build-up: line marking, EPDM spray coat, SBR base layer, primer, and asphalt or concrete base.

Sandwich Track System

A sealed, water-resistant system designed for intensive use. A cushioned SBR layer is protected by a polyurethane seal and finished with a durable EPDM surface, providing an effective balance of comfort, resilience and longevity. Typical build-up: line marking, EPDM top layer, polyurethane seal layer, SBR base layer, primer, and asphalt or concrete base.

Full-Pour Track System - IAAF Certified

A premium, seamless polyurethane system for competition and professional athletics. Multiple resilient layers provide consistent energy return, traction and long-term performance under demanding use. This Full-Pour Track System is IAAF certified. Certification covers the product, system build-up and installation requirements specified in the applicable certificate. Typical build-up: line marking, EPDM top layer, polyurethane resilient layers, primer, and asphalt or concrete base.

Prefabricated Track System - IAAF Certified

A factory-engineered roll system offering uniform thickness, fast installation and reliable performance. It is ideal for projects where predictable quality and efficient delivery are priorities. This Prefabricated Track System is IAAF certified. Certification covers the product, system build-up and installation requirements specified in the applicable certificate. Typical build-up: line marking, prefabricated track layer, adhesive binder, and asphalt or concrete base.

Choosing the Right Track

The best system depends on the facility type, expected usage, local climate, performance goals, maintenance plan and project budget. HKS Surfaces can review your requirements and recommend a suitable system for your project.
