Multi-Sport Court Flooring Solutions

Professional multi-sport court flooring systems designed for facilities where one court is used for several sports. Our systems provide reliable grip, consistent ball response and long-lasting performance for both indoor and outdoor applications.

Flooring Options

Acrylic Sports Flooring - Durable & Weather Resistant

A textured sports surface ideal for outdoor and high-use multi-purpose courts. Excellent grip and ball response, UV and weather resistance, low maintenance, and custom court colours and line markings. Best for: Basketball, tennis, pickleball, volleyball and recreational courts.

Polyurethane (PU) Sports Flooring - Comfort & Performance

A seamless resilient system providing greater shock absorption and player comfort. Excellent shock absorption, comfortable underfoot, seamless finish, and easy to clean and maintain. Best for: Indoor basketball, badminton, volleyball and multi-purpose sports halls.

One Court - Multiple Sports

Different sports can be incorporated into a single surface using colour-coded playing zones and precision line markings, allowing facilities to maximise available space.

Schools • Universities • Sports Clubs • Community Centres • Hotels & Resorts • Residential Developments • Multi-Sport Facilities
