Padel Court Flooring Solutions

Professional padel court flooring solutions for indoor and outdoor facilities, with Artificial Grass, Polyurethane (PU) and Acrylic systems.

Artificial Grass

The Popular Choice

Short-pile synthetic turf with silica sand infill provides consistent ball response, comfortable movement and reliable grip.

Best for: Padel clubs, hotels, resorts and residential courts.

  • Comfortable & controlled play
  • Durable & low maintenance
  • Indoor & outdoor use

Polyurethane (PU)

Comfort & Multi-Use

A seamless, resilient surface offering excellent shock absorption and player comfort, particularly for indoor and multi-purpose facilities.

Best for: Indoor courts, schools, clubs and multi-sport facilities.

  • Excellent shock absorption
  • Comfortable underfoot
  • Easy to clean & maintain

Acrylic

Fast & Responsive

A durable court system providing a firmer, faster playing experience. Surface texture can be adjusted to achieve the required grip and playing speed.

Best for: Outdoor courts and multi-sport facilities.

  • Fast & responsive play
  • Adjustable grip & speed
  • Durable & weather resistant
