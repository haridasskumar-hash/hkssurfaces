Gym Flooring Solutions

Professional gym and fitness flooring solutions designed to protect subfloors, reduce impact and noise, improve grip and provide a comfortable surface for training areas.

Our Gym Flooring Solutions

Rubber Flooring Rolls - Seamless & Versatile

Durable rubber rolls create a smooth, professional surface with fewer joints and excellent resistance to daily wear. Benefits: shock and sound absorption, slip-resistant surface, durable and easy to maintain, clean professional appearance. Best for: Commercial gyms, fitness centres, cardio zones and functional training areas.

Rubber Gym Tiles - Heavy-Duty Protection

High-density rubber tiles provide excellent impact protection and can be easily installed or replaced individually. Benefits: excellent impact absorption, protects concrete subfloors, modular and easy to replace, suitable for heavy equipment. Best for: Free-weight zones, strength training areas, home gyms and fitness facilities.

EPDM Rubber Flooring - Colourful & Customizable

Seamless EPDM rubber flooring combines performance with greater design flexibility, allowing colours, patterns and zones to be incorporated into the floor. Benefits: comfortable and resilient, custom colour options, slip-resistant, seamless finish. Best for: Fitness studios, functional training areas, premium gyms and multi-purpose exercise spaces.
