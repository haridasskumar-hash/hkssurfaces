Our Solutions

Acrylic Sports Flooring

A durable, textured system offering consistent ball response, excellent grip and strong weather resistance. Benefits: consistent ball bounce, UV and weather resistance, durable and low maintenance, multiple court colours, and suitable for new courts and resurfacing. Best for: Clubs, schools, hotels, resorts, residential courts and commercial facilities.

Cushioned Acrylic System

A multi-layer system with resilient cushion layers for improved comfort and impact reduction. Benefits: enhanced shock absorption, greater player comfort, consistent ball response, slip-resistant finish, UV and weather resistance, and custom colours. Best for: Premium clubs, academies, hotels, resorts and comfort-focused facilities.

Typical Court System

  • Prepared concrete or asphalt base
  • Primer or base coat
  • Acrylic resurfacer
  • Optional cushion layers
  • Textured acrylic colour coats
  • Precision court line marking
