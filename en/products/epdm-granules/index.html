Green EPDM Granules

Green EPDM granules supplied by HKS Surfaces are manufactured from selected virgin rubber materials using advanced granulating technology to provide consistent granule sizing, efficient installation and long-term flooring performance.

Key Features

  • Made from virgin EPDM rubber materials
  • Available in 21 vibrant colors
  • Excellent color stability
  • Peroxide-cured EPDM
  • Consistent granule sizing
  • Flexible and resilient surface
  • Excellent impact-absorption properties
  • Non-toxic and environmentally friendly materials
  • Suitable for creative color combinations and custom designs
  • Designed for professional wet-pour rubber flooring systems

EPDM Granule Sizes

Our team can recommend the appropriate granule size and specification according to the application and flooring system.

0.5-1.5 mm

Suitable for specialized surfacing requirements and applications requiring finer granules.

1.0-4.0 mm

A commonly used size for EPDM wet-pour flooring and recreational surfacing.
