EPDM Rubber Safety Flooring

HKS Surfaces provides professional EPDM Rubber Safety Flooring designed for water parks, splash pads, aquatic playgrounds, swimming pool surrounds and other wet-play environments.

Our seamless rubber flooring system combines a shock-absorbing SBR rubber base layer with a durable coloured EPDM rubber top layer, creating a comfortable, resilient and slip-resistant surface suitable for areas exposed to water and outdoor conditions.

Key Benefits

  • Slip-resistant surface for wet areas
  • Impact absorbing for improved safety
  • Porous system for effective water drainage
  • UV & weather resistant
  • Comfortable underfoot
  • Seamless and easy to maintain
  • Wide range of EPDM colours
  • Custom patterns, graphics & themed designs

Typical EPDM Safety Flooring System

EPDM Colour Wear Layer

Durable, UV-resistant coloured rubber finish.

SBR Shock-Absorbing Base Layer

Provides cushioning and impact protection.

Prepared Concrete Base

Stable foundation with suitable drainage and falls.
