High-Performance Basketball Court Surfaces

HKS Surfaces provides professional basketball court flooring systems designed for schools, sports facilities, residential developments, community courts and commercial sports projects.

Our outdoor court systems combine a durable prepared base with multiple acrylic coating layers to create a smooth, consistent and weather-resistant playing surface.

Key Benefits

  • Excellent ball bounce and consistent play
  • Anti-slip textured surface
  • UV and weather resistant
  • Durable for high-traffic sports use
  • Comfortable underfoot
  • Low maintenance
  • Wide range of court colours
  • Suitable for indoor and outdoor courts

Basketball Court System

1. Concrete / Asphalt Base

Stable, properly levelled foundation with suitable drainage.

2. Acrylic Resurfacer

Helps fill minor surface imperfections and creates a uniform base.

3. Cushion Layer - Optional

Multiple resilient layers can be added for improved player comfort and shock absorption.

4. Acrylic Colour Coating

Durable textured sports coating providing colour, grip and weather resistance.

5. Line Marking

Professional basketball court markings applied according to the required court layout.

Standard or Cushioned System

Standard Acrylic System

A durable and economical solution for schools, residential projects, community courts and recreational facilities.

Cushioned Acrylic System

Adds resilient cushioning beneath the colour coating for greater player comfort. Recommended for premium sports facilities and courts with frequent use.

Basketball Courts | Multi-Sport Courts | Schools | Universities | Residential Developments | Hotels & Resorts | Sports Clubs | Community Facilities
