What Is EPDM Flooring and Where Is It Used?

An introduction to EPDM flooring, its key properties and typical applications.

2026-08-21

What is EPDM?

EPDM is a synthetic rubber widely used as the wearing layer in rubber surfacing systems, particularly in playgrounds and recreational areas.

Key advantages

EPDM supports custom colours, zoning and graphics, and can be combined with impact-absorbing layers according to project requirements.
